About Us

AUTOWARE SOL TECH is a Canadian procurement and sourcing company dedicated to helping industrial clients obtain quality engineering materials and difficult-to-source spare parts from North America. We support maintenance shutdowns, project procurement, plant upgrades and emergency sourcing requirements.


Mission

To provide reliable industrial procurement services that reduce downtime and improve operational efficiency.

Vision

To become a trusted global sourcing partner for oil & gas, mining and industrial service companies.

Core Values

Integrity, responsiveness, quality sourcing, customer satisfaction and long-term partnerships.
